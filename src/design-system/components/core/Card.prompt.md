**Card** — a warm paper surface with a soft shadow for grouping content.

```jsx
<Card accent="top" interactive>
  <h3>학술 세미나</h3>
  <p>매주 진행되는 정기 세미나…</p>
</Card>
```

- `interactive` adds a 4px hover lift + deeper shadow (use for clickable cards).
- `accent="top"` draws a 3px red rule across the top edge.
- `padding`: `sm` / `md` / `lg`.

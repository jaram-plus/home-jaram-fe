**SectionHeader** — the signature JARAM heading: uppercase eyebrow, a title with a red highlight phrase, and an optional lead paragraph.

```jsx
<SectionHeader
  eyebrow="What We Do"
  title="함께 성장하는"
  highlight="3가지 방법"
  description="체계적인 프로그램으로 여러분의 성장을 지원합니다"
/>
```

- `font`: `sans` (bold UI title) · `serif` / `display` (editorial, larger, lighter).
- `align`: `center` (default) or `left`. `highlight` always renders in brand red.
